# IJGGC V2 intercoder reliability and sensitivity report

## Review universe

- Claim-eligible papers independently recoded: 57.
- Paper-field items in the combined matrix: 627.
- Directly comparable items: 589.
- Exact pre-adjudication agreements: 139 (23.6%).
- Author-adjudicated disagreements: 450.
- Baseline-unavailable items independently verified: 38.

These diagnostics show substantial interpretation burden. The manuscript does not characterize the agreement as high.

## Ordered-score reliability

| Dimension | Numeric pairs | Linear weighted kappa | Quadratic weighted kappa |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Field evidence | 54 | 0.519 | 0.594 |
| Physical consistency | 43 | 0.121 | 0.187 |
| Uncertainty | 27 | 0.239 | 0.400 |
| Transferability | 23 | 0.162 | 0.258 |
| Decision readiness | 54 | 0.281 | 0.340 |

Non-numeric states such as `not reported`, `unclear`, and `not applicable` were excluded from weighted calculations rather than converted to zero. Unweighted field-level Cohen's kappa values ranged from 0.002 to 0.413.

| Surrogate-error propagation | 4 | 0 | 4 | 0 |
| Physical-consistency score at least 3 | 42 | 1 | 33 | 8 |
| Decision-readiness score at least 3 | 11 | 2 | 9 | 0 |

The exact-agreement column is a conservative lower-bound diagnostic, not an alternative estimate of field prevalence. Results that depend strongly on adjudication are explicitly identified in the manuscript.

## Remaining scientific gate

The full quantitative universe has now been independently recoded, but the low initial agreement and high adjudication dependence remain material limitations. Before submission, the authors should confirm that the revised codebook is acceptable and decide whether a second calibration round or third-person adjudication is scientifically necessary. Automated validation cannot provide that author judgment.
